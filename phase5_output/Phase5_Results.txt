HMM PARAMETERS
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Transition matrix A (designed; from \ to) — the spec's illustrative matrix forgets
degradation within one quiet turn, which is fatal with sparse keyword hits:
                    Healthy   Slipping   Failed
    Healthy           0.94      0.05      0.01
    Slipping          0.08      0.85      0.07
    Failed            0.05      0.05      0.90
Initial distribution pi = [Healthy 0.95, Slipping 0.045, Failed 0.005]
Emission matrix B — P(Error Found | state), DESIGNED per the phase spec's
qualitative direction (errors indicate degradation). See the ceiling
analysis below for WHY they are not fitted to the data:
    Healthy  = [0.86 NoErr, 0.14 Err]
    Slipping = [0.50 NoErr, 0.50 Err]
    Failed   = [0.10 NoErr, 0.90 Err]

INTERPRETATION
----------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Phase 5 extends the project from post-mortem attribution (who broke?) to
temporal monitoring (who is degrading — and can we say so BEFORE the crash?).
The HMM converts a stream of noisy keyword flags into a drifting health
belief per agent, and the sweep above shows how often that belief crosses
the 0.50 danger threshold strictly before the ground-truth mistake round.

Honest reading of the numbers:
  1. mistake_step marks the FIRST faulty message, and the mistake is a
     reasoning error: keywords virtually never appear BEFORE it
     (P(Err|pre)=0.087 vs P(Err|at)=0.198). A warning AT the mistake round
     is counted as NOT early here — a strict yardstick; the lenient count
     (early + at-the-round) is also reported, and the ceiling analysis
     shows what any detector could achieve on this evidence channel.
  2. 58/91 files have their mistake at round 1 — structurally unpredictable.
     Early-warning accuracy must be read among the 33 evaluable files.
  3. Agents' HMMs are independent given the shared turn axis; there is no
     cross-agent coupling — a deliberate simplification that keeps the
     forward pass exact and O(T) per agent.
  4. Transition/prior matrices are spec-tuned and emissions derive from the
     Phase 3 learned sensor CPTs; no EM fitting (Baum-Welch) was run —
     that is the natural next step.
  5. Richer features (code-execution failures, tool errors, answer-flip
     detection) would raise the ceiling substantially; keywords alone cap
     strict earliness at the level reported above.
