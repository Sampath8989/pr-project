NOTE — why the probabilities now differ per file:
  Each agent's keyword sensor injects instance-level evidence, so the
  posterior P(Ai Failed | evidence) is file-specific. Sensors keep the
  network a tree, so VE remains exact; the brute-force cross-check over
  the 2^10 joint confirmed agreement to 2.220e-16.
  When S3 is missing (deleted logs), VE simply never clamps S3 and
  marginalizes over it — inference stays well-defined and exact.
  On files where NO agent's logs contain any keyword, the posterior
  collapses to the learned no-evidence prior (identical rows).
  Learned sensor CPTs: keyword hits are positively correlated with failure
  for A1, anti-correlated for A2..A4 (downstream agents echo the fallout
  of upstream mistakes), and VE exploits those likelihood ratios.
